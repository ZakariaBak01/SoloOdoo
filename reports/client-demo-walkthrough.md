## 20-minute flow

1. Open **EL MOKRIF → Chantiers → Atlas Marina Residence**. Show its In Progress status, Atlas Developments customer, Atlas Marina Site, plan, warehouse, and team. Explain this is the project record joining commercial, operational and financial work.
2. Open the **Tasks** tab. Show completed mobilisation/foundations and open concrete-structure/MEP tasks. Progress is based on operational work.
3. Open **Construction Estimates → Atlas Marina — Approved Cost Plan**. Show quantities, material/labour/machinery assumptions, approved status, actual costs and forecast. The approved estimate is a protected baseline; changes use revisions.
4. Open **CRM → Atlas Phase II Extension**, then **Sales → Orders → Structural Works Package**. Show the confirmed order and chantier link.
5. Open **Material Requests** for the Atlas Marina Cement 50 kg and Reinforcement Steel request, then **Purchase → Orders** for Maroc Matériaux. Explain traceability from site demand through purchasing, quality, transfer and consumption.
6. Open **Bills of Quantities → Atlas Marina Contract BOQ**. Explain it controls quantities, variations and progress certification.
7. In construction-control menus, show **Podium concrete structure** work package, **Slab reinforcement clarification** RFI, **Waterproofing technical submittal**, **Lobby finish upgrade request**, and **VO-001 Lobby finish upgrade**. Then open **Atlas Marina concrete supply tender**.
8. Open **Documents → Approved Structural Drawing — Podium Slab** to demonstrate controlled evidence. Open **Finance → Finance Readiness**, then finish on **Management Dashboard** for the executive summary.
