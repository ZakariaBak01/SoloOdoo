# UAT-22 — BOQ preparation, tender, evaluation, and award

**Users:** U2 BOQ estimator, U4 BOQ reviewer/tender evaluator/award approver, U1 BOQ approver/tender manager, U3 tender buyer. **Input:** `CH-UAT-001`, BOQ `UAT Foundation BOQ`, package `FOUND`, line `03.01.001 Concrete slab` 10 m³ at 500 MAD contract rate; bidders `UAT Supplier A` and a separate `UAT Supplier B`. **Prerequisite:** UAT-01. Run BOQ preparation before UAT-18/20/21.

1. U2: open **EL MOKRIF → Tendering & BOQ → Bills of Quantities → New**. Enter title, chantier, **Purpose** client contract or the relevant available choice, package code `FOUND`. On **WBS → Add a line**, enter code `03` and name `Foundation`.
2. On **BOQ Lines → Add a line**, enter item code `03.01.001`, description `Concrete slab`, WBS section, unit m³, **Quantity Method** Manual, **Manual Quantity** `10`. In its **Rate Analysis**, add material/labour/equipment components with unit costs; on **Commercial** enter overhead, contingency, margin and **Contract Unit Rate** `500`. Attach a dummy drawing; click **Save** and record totals.
3. U2: click **Submit for Review**. U4: inspect quantities, drawing and rates; click **Request Changes**, enter reason `Confirm slab depth`, then **Request Changes** in the wizard. U2 corrects evidence and resubmits. U4 clicks **Complete Review**; U1 clicks **Approve Baseline**. Verify approved BOQ is current and readonly. This BOQ is the input for UAT-18/20/21.
4. U3: open **Tendering & BOQ → Tenders → New**. Enter `UAT Foundation Tender`, chantier, approved BOQ, tender type, package code `FOUND`, buyer U3, manager U1, evaluation committee U4, clarification/submission deadlines. On **Evaluation Matrix**, set technical/commercial/delivery/risk weights to total 100 and add at least one mandatory criterion. Click **Save**.
5. U1: click **Approve for Issue → Issue Tender**. U3: on **Invited Bidders**, create bids for Supplier A and B. Open each bid, click **Load BOQ**, enter quoted quantity `10`, unit rates `480` and `520` MAD, delivery days, validity, exclusions, and attach each test quotation. Click **Submit Bid**.
6. U1: click **Close Bids → Start Technical Evaluation**. U4: open each bid under **Tender Bids**, score criteria and enter comments, click **Complete Technical Evaluation**. Verify a noncompliant bid cannot win solely on price.
7. U1: click **Start Commercial Evaluation → Build Comparison**. U4: open **Bid Comparisons** and check normalized totals, scores, rank, and completeness. U3: on the selected compliant bid click **Prepare Award**, enter **Recommendation Reason** and award line quantity/rate, click **Submit Recommendation**.
8. U4: open **Awards**, inspect comparison and evidence, click **Approve Award**. U3: click **Create Purchase Order** on the award. Verify one PO with the chosen supplier, BOQ line, chantier and approved rate; click again if offered and verify no duplicate.

**Pass:** U2/U4/U1 approval roles stay separate; tender issue and award are controlled; comparison uses complete compliant bids; one approved award creates one linked PO.
