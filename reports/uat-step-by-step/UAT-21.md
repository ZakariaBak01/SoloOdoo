# UAT-21 — Progress certificate, invoice, and project-control totals

**Users:** U8 prepares certificate and invoice; U4 verifies measurement; U1 approves. **Input:** approved client-contract BOQ from UAT-22, two non-overlapping periods, contracted quantity 10 units, first period measured 2, second period measured 3, retention 5%, advance recovery 10%. **Prerequisite:** approved BOQ and Finance Readiness UAT-10.

1. U8: open **EL MOKRIF → Project Control → Progress Certificates → New**. Select Company A, `CH-UAT-001`, current approved client-contract BOQ, **Billing Product** `Construction Service`, and first period dates; click **Save → Load BOQ Lines**. Enter **Current Quantity** `2`, attach measurement evidence, click **Save → Submit**. U4 clicks **Verify Measurement**; U1 enters customer reference `UAT-PC-000` and clicks **Approve Certificate**.
2. U8: click **New** again. Select the same chantier/BOQ/billing product and a second, non-overlapping period; click **Save → Load BOQ Lines**. Verify read-only **Previous Quantity** is now `2`; it is calculated from the prior approved certificate. Set **Current Quantity** to `9` and save; record the over-contract rejection. Restore **Current Quantity** to `3`, which makes read-only **Cumulative Quantity** `5`.
3. Enter **Retention** `5%`, **Advance Recovery** `10%`, compare gross/current/net figures with a calculator, and attach measurement evidence. Click **Save → Submit**. U8 tries **Verify Measurement** on own certificate; record segregation denial. U4 opens it and clicks **Verify Measurement**. U1 records customer certificate reference `UAT-PC-001` and clicks **Approve Certificate**.
4. U8: click **Create Invoice**. Open invoice using **Open Invoice**; verify one draft customer invoice, the chantier, customer, lines, deductions and analytic account. Click **Create Invoice** again if offered; there must still be one linked invoice. Post only after confirming Finance Readiness is valid.
5. U1: open the chantier **Project Control** tab. Record Original Budget, approved changes, Current Budget, Actual Cost, Forecast Cost, Original/Current Contract, Certified Revenue and forecast margin. Trace each amount to estimate, BOQ, variation, certificate, invoice or source cost.
6. U13: open **Management Dashboard**, compare active/At Risk/Off Track chantiers, issues, overdue RFIs, pending changes, approved variation cost and certified revenue to source lists. U14 checks Company B isolation.

**Pass:** cumulative quantity stays within contract, deductions are transparent, submitter/verifier/approver are distinct, invoice creation is idempotent, and totals trace to source records.
